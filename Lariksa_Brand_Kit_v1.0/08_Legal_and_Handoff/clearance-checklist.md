# Pre-launch checks - not legal advice

**Important**: Lariksa, its wordmark and monogram are speculative identity concepts. No global name availability, trademark ownership, domain ownership, or logo originality has been certified.

Before public launch:
1. Search the exact name **Lariksa** and phonetically/visually similar names in WIPO Global Brand Database and national registries where you operate; use a qualified trademark attorney.
2. Check relevant classes, including Nice 9 for downloadable apps and 42 for SaaS, plus any other goods/services.
3. Check App Store, Play Store, GitHub, social usernames, business registries, relevant domain registries and search engines.
4. Confirm the logo's distinctive character, icon similarity, and type licensing.
5. Obtain domains and handles only after clearance, and record ownership centrally.
6. Conduct WCAG contrast testing and usability testing on actual screen contexts.

Official starting points:
- https://branddb.wipo.int/
- https://pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id/
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono

No font binaries are included. Download fonts from the official provider and comply with their licenses.
